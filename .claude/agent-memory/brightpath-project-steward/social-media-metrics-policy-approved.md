**Verbatim policy — SOCIAL MEDIA / PORTFOLIO METRICS POLICY (approved):**

BrightPath distinguishes between durable outcome metrics and time-sensitive marketing
analytics.

1. Durable before/after outcomes from completed website work may be surfaced prominently
   as trust evidence when they are verified and accurately represent the work. Examples
   currently include: AweStruck Intelligence (Lighthouse mobile 26 → 100) and Angel City
   Massage (Lighthouse desktop 62 → 99 / mobile 62 → 97).

2. Social-media analytics such as impressions, reach, engagement, follower counts, likes,
   comments, and month-over-month growth should NOT be presented prominently as though
   they are current/live BrightPath results. These figures change over time and would
   create unnecessary ongoing maintenance.

3. Historical social-media metrics may remain within detailed case studies when useful as
   evidence, but they must: be accurate and verified, be clearly tied to a specific
   reporting/campaign period, and be presented as a historical snapshot rather than
   current performance. Example framing: "30-Day Campaign Snapshot — April 20–May 19,
   2026."

4. Social-media portfolio teasers should primarily emphasize durable work and
   capabilities — building a social presence from the ground up, strategy and content
   planning, reels/video and visual content, captions/storytelling, community engagement,
   scheduling and ongoing management — rather than volatile monthly statistics.

5. Do not create a BrightPath maintenance requirement where social-media portfolio
   numbers need to be manually updated every month.

6. This decision does NOT validate any currently questionable metrics. Living Better
   Life's contradictory figures and Dale Tiffany's unsupported claims remain unresolved
   evidence-integrity issues and must not be treated as verified merely because
   historical snapshots are permitted.

**Why (relayed context, not part of the verbatim policy itself):** a read-only
trust-evidence audit across the homepage `PortfolioSection`, Portfolio cards, case-study
data files, and the Reviews system surfaced two unresolved evidence-integrity problems
that prompted this policy. Those findings are **not resolved by this policy** (see point
6 above, restated) and are tracked separately in
[[trust-evidence-integrity-open-issues]] — do not treat that file's contents as fixed or
verified just because this policy exists.

**How to apply:** When reviewing any future plan/diff touching the homepage portfolio
teaser, Portfolio cards, or case-study social-media metrics, check it against points 1-5
above. Flag any proposal that would (a) present volatile social analytics as current/live
results, (b) present unlabeled/undated historical social numbers, or (c) introduce a
recurring manual-update burden for social metrics. Also flag, per point 6, any proposal
that treats the Living Better Life or Dale Tiffany numbers as already-fixed just because
this policy was approved — the policy sets the rule going forward, it does not itself
correct those two files. This steward does not implement fixes to case-study data; that
is main-session/coding-agent work.
